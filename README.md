# 🚀 Global Space Missions Analysis

### From the first launches to the future of space.

Space exploration is not just about rockets and launches. Behind every mission are countries, organizations, technology, failures, breakthroughs, AI and a growing space economy.

**Global Space Missions Analysis** turns that journey into an interactive data story — exploring how space exploration has changed and where it could be heading next.

## 🌌 What You will Explore

### 🌍 Space Missions

How launch activity, mission outcomes, countries and organizations have changed over time.

### 🚀 Rocket Evolution

How launch vehicles and rocket technology have developed alongside space exploration.

### 🤖 AI in Space

How AI, autonomy and intelligent systems are becoming part of modern space exploration.

### 💰 Space Economy

How space has grown from a scientific frontier into an increasingly important economic one.

## 📊 From Data to Dashboard

```text
Raw Data → Cleaning → Analysis → Visualization → Insights
```

The repository keeps the **raw and cleaned datasets separately**, making the data journey easier to follow.
